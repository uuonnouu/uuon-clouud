Unlike official constellations, which are formally defined regions of the sky with boundaries, asterisms are informal star patterns that are often culturally significant and used for navigation, storytelling, or seasonal markers.     * Three bright stars in a row in the constellation Orion.

    * A small but iconic asterism in the Southern Hemisphere.
