**SECTION 1: What is the Data Crisis?**  
The world is facing an unprecedented explosion in data generation. This includes everything from personal data to enterprise logs, AI training sets, and blockchain records. The core problems include:  
	•	**Overcapacity**: Storage systems are being overwhelmed.  
	•	**Rising costs**: Energy and infrastructure costs for data centers are rising.  
	•	**Security failures**: Backup systems are inconsistent, with poor data verification.  
	•	**Environmental impact**: Data centers contribute heavily to global emissions.

**SECTION 2: Verified Facts and Data**  
	1.	**Data Overload**  
	•	Global data volume is forecast to reach over **400 zettabytes by 2028**.  
	•	[Source: www.techradar.com/pro](https://www.techradar.com/pro/2025-business-priorities-tackling-the-data-crunch-and-storage-crisis)  
	2.	**Enterprise Strain**  
	•	Average organizations store over **150 petabytes**, and this number is doubling every ~3 years.  
	•	[Source: www.itpro.com](https://www.itpro.com/hardware/storage/ai-is-causing-a-data-storage-crisis-for-enterprises)  
	3.	**Rising Complexity**  
	•	67% of companies are offloading data storage to reduce operational complexity.  
	•	[Source: www.okoone.com](https://www.okoone.com/spark/strategy-transformation/how-enterprises-are-tackling-data-overload-with-hybrid-storage-solutions)  
	4.	**Cyberattack Risks**  
	•	98% of companies using hybrid cloud have experienced at least one serious data breach.  
	•	[Source: www.b2bdaily.com](https://b2bdaily.com/it/hybrid-cloud-security-crisis-tackling-cyber-threats-in-2025)  
	5.	**Backup Failures**  
	•	30% of enterprises report that their backups fail to restore correctly.  
	•	[Source: www.cybernoz.com](https://cybernoz.com/bcdr-trends-and-challenges-for-2025)  
	6.	**Environmental Costs**  
	•	Data centers may produce more carbon emissions than the airline industry by 2030.  
	•	[Source: Reddit Technology Forum, summarized from Climate Reports]
