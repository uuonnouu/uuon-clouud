# Geomet Art Principles/Manipulation Prompts  
  
Area Formulas  
	1.	Rectangle: A = length × width  
	2.	Square: A = side²  
	3.	Triangle: A = ½ × base × height  
	4.	Circle: A = πr²  
	5.	Trapezoid: A = ½(b₁ + b₂) × height  
	6.	Parallelogram: A = base × height  
	7.	Rhombus: A = ½ × d₁ × d₂ (where d₁, d₂ are diagonals)  
	8.	Ellipse: A = πab (where a, b are semi-axes)  
Perimeter/Circumference Formulas  
	9.	Rectangle: P = 2(length + width)  
	10.	Square: P = 4 × side  
	11.	Triangle: P = a + b + c  
	12.	Circle: C = 2πr or C = πd  
Volume Formulas  
	13.	Cube: V = side³  
	14.	Rectangular prism: V = length × width × height  
	15.	Cylinder: V = πr²h  
	16.	Sphere: V = (4/3)πr³  
	17.	Cone: V = (1/3)πr²h  
	18.	Pyramid: V = (1/3) × base area × height  
Surface Area Formulas  
	19.	Cube: SA = 6 × side²  
	20.	Rectangular prism: SA = 2(lw + lh + wh)  
	21.	Cylinder: SA = 2πr² + 2πrh  
	22.	Sphere: SA = 4πr²  
	23.	Cone: SA = πr² + πrs (where s is slant height)  
Special Formulas  
	24.	Pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c²  
	25.	Distance formula: d = √[(x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²]  
These formulas cover the most commonly used geometric calculations for 2D shapes, 3D solids, and coordinate geometry that you’ll encounter in mathematics, engineering, and practical applications.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​  
  
Animation Types  
Rotation & Spin  
	•	Continuous rotation around multiple axes (not just one)  
	•	Variable rotation speeds for organic feel  
	•	Wobble effects by slightly varying rotation speed  
	•	Precession (rotation axis itself rotates)  
Scaling & Breathing  
	•	Rhythmic scaling up/down to simulate “breathing”  
	•	Non-uniform scaling (stretch in one direction)  
	•	Pulse effects synchronized with other elements  
Position Animation  
	•	Orbital motion around invisible points  
	•	Figure-8 or Lissajous curve paths  
	•	Floating/bobbing motions with sine waves  
	•	Particle-like swarm behaviors  
Advanced Dynamics  
Morphing & Deformation  
	•	Vertex displacement using noise functions  
	•	Shape tweening between different geometries  
	•	Elastic deformations when “colliding” with boundaries  
	•	Procedural geometry that evolves over time  
Physics Simulation  
	•	Gravity effects and bouncing  
	•	Spring systems for connected shapes  
	•	Collision detection between objects  
	•	Fluid-like motion with momentum and inertia  
Interactive Responses  
	•	Mouse/cursor attraction or repulsion  
	•	Audio-reactive scaling or color changes  
	•	Touch/click triggered animations  
	•	Camera movement following or orbiting shapes  
Visual Enhancement  
Material Dynamics  
	•	Animated textures and normal maps  
	•	Color cycling through HSV space  
	•	Transparency/opacity animations  
	•	Reflectivity and refraction changes  
Lighting Effects  
	•	Moving light sources  
	•	Color-changing illumination  
	•	Particle systems for sparkles/trails  
	•	Volumetric lighting and fog  
Environmental Interaction  
	•	Shadows that respond to moving lights  
	•	Reflections in other surfaces  
	•	Particle trails following movement  
	•	Environmental mapping that changes  
Implementation Tips  
Smooth Motion  
	•	Use easing functions (ease-in, ease-out)  
	•	Combine multiple sine waves for complex motion  
	•	Add small random variations to avoid mechanical feel  
	•	Use time-based animation for consistent speed  
Performance Optimization  
	•	Level-of-detail systems for distant objects  
	•	Frustum culling for off-screen shapes  
	•	Instancing for multiple similar objects  
	•	Efficient update loops with delta time
