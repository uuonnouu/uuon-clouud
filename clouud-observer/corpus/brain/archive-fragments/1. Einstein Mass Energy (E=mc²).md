1. **Einstein Mass Energy** (E=mc²)  
* **Formula**: E = mc²  
* **Summary**: The fundamental mass-energy equivalence equation showing that mass and energy are interchangeable. Visualizes energy release from mass conversion.  
2. **Einstein Energy Per Mass** (E/m = c²)  
* **Formula**: E/m = c²  
* **Summary**: Energy density per unit mass equals the speed of light squared. Shows the enormous energy contained in matter.  
3. **Einstein Mass From Energy** (m = E/c²)  
* **Formula**: m = E/c²  
* **Summary**: Mass can be calculated from energy content. Demonstrates how energy has effective mass (relativistic mass concept).  
4. **Einstein Multiplicative Form** (E × m = m²c²)  
* **Formula**: E × m = m²c²  
* **Summary**: Alternative multiplicative representation of mass-energy relationship showing the squared mass term.  
5. **Einstein Absolute Magnitude** (|E| = |m|c²)  
* **Formula**: |E| = |m|c²  
* **Summary**: Absolute value form ensuring energy magnitude equals mass magnitude times c². Used for antimatter calculations.  
6. **Einstein Tensor Product** (E ⊗ m tensor)  
* **Formula**: E ⊗ m = (mc²) ⊗ m tensor form  
* **Summary**: Tensor representation of mass-energy for spacetime calculations and general relativity applications.  
7. **Einstein Dot Product** (E · m vector)  
* **Formula**: E · m = mc² (vector dot product)  
* **Summary**: Vector formulation used in momentum-energy 4-vector calculations in special relativity.  
8. **Einstein Exponential Form** (E = mc² × exp terms)  
* **Formula**: E = mc² × e^(kinetic terms)  
* **Summary**: Extended form including relativistic corrections for high-velocity particles approaching light speed.  
9. **Relativistic Energy Momentum** (E² = (pc)² + (mc²)²)  
* **Formula**: E² = (pc)² + (mc²)²  
* **Summary**: Complete relativistic energy equation including both rest mass energy and momentum energy components.
