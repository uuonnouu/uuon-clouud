Here’s a progression of geometric shapes and concepts organized by typical grade school learning levels:  
  
## Kindergarten - Grade 2 (Ages 5-8)  
  
**Basic 2D Shapes:**  
  
- Circle  
- Square  
- Triangle  
- Rectangle  
- Oval/Ellipse  
  
**Simple Patterns:**  
  
- Straight lines  
- Zigzag lines  
- Wavy lines  
- Dots in rows  
  
## Grade 3-4 (Ages 8-10)  
  
**Extended 2D Shapes:**  
  
- Pentagon (5 sides)  
- Hexagon (6 sides)  
- Octagon (8 sides)  
- Diamond/Rhombus  
- Parallelogram  
  
**Basic 3D Shapes:**  
  
- Cube  
- Sphere  
- Cylinder  
- Cone  
- Rectangular prism (box)  
  
**Simple Geometric Patterns:**  
  
- Tessellations with squares and triangles  
- Basic symmetry (reflection)  
  
## Grade 5-6 (Ages 10-12)  
  
**More Complex 2D Shapes:**  
  
- Trapezoid  
- Kite  
- Regular polygons up to 12 sides  
- Star shapes (5-pointed, 6-pointed)  
  
**Advanced 3D Shapes:**  
  
- Pyramid (square base)  
- Triangular prism  
- Pentagonal prism  
- Oval/ellipsoid (3D oval)  
  
**Coordinate Geometry Basics:**  
  
- Points on a grid  
- Simple line graphs  
- Basic plotting  
  
## Grade 7-8 (Ages 12-14)  
  
**Geometric Constructions:**  
  
- Perpendicular lines  
- Parallel lines  
- Angle bisectors  
- Circle constructions with compass  
  
**Advanced 3D Concepts:**  
  
- Prisms of various polygon bases  
- Pyramids of various bases  
- Understanding faces, edges, vertices  
  
**Basic Transformations:**  
  
- Rotation (90°, 180°, 270°)  
- Reflection across axes  
- Translation (sliding)  
- Basic scaling  
  
## Mathematical Progression for Visualization:  
  
**Level 1 - Parametric Circles and Lines:**  
  
```  
Circle: x = r*cos(t), y = r*sin(t)  
Line: y = mx + b  
```  
  
**Level 2 - Simple Polygons:**  
  
```  
Regular n-gon: vertices at equal angles around circle  
Square: 4 equal sides, 90° angles  
Triangle: 3 sides, various types  
```  
  
**Level 3 - Basic 3D Extrusions:**  
  
```  
Cube: square extruded in z-direction  
Cylinder: circle extruded in z-direction  
Prism: any polygon extruded in z-direction  
```  
  
**Level 4 - Rotational Shapes:**  
  
```  
Sphere: circle rotated around diameter  
Cone: triangle rotated around one edge  
Torus: circle rotated around external axis  
```  
  
**Level 5 - Coordinate Transformations:**  
  
```  
Translation: (x,y) → (x+a, y+b)  
Rotation: (x,y) → (x*cos(θ)-y*sin(θ), x*sin(θ)+y*cos(θ))  
Scaling: (x,y) → (ax, by)  
```  
  
## Recommended Learning Sequence for 3D Visualization:  
  
1. **Start with 2D shapes** - circles, squares, triangles  
1. **Add simple extrusion** - turn 2D shapes into 3D by adding depth  
1. **Introduce rotation** - spin 2D shapes around axes  
1. **Basic transformations** - move, rotate, scale shapes  
1. **Combine shapes** - create complex objects from simple parts  
1. **Add patterns** - tessellations, fractals, repetitions  
  
This progression follows how spatial reasoning develops naturally, starting with recognition of basic shapes and building up to understanding complex 3D transformations and mathematical relationships.
