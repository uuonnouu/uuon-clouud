# Π. I   
  
**The Grand Misdirection: How the Greeks Were Fooled into Translating a False History**  
  
The year is about **2,300 years ago**. The city of **Alexandria, Egypt**, is booming—a powerhouse of knowledge and also home to the **Great Library**. Ruled by **Greek elites** who believe they are the masters of the known world. Their leader, **Ptolemy II**, sits on the throne, a man obsessed with controlling information. He’s got an empire to run, and what better way to maintain power than to **own history itself**?  
  
But there’s a problem.  
  
See, there’s a group of people living in Alexandria that don’t quite fit in with the Greek world—**the Jews**. They don’t worship the Greek gods. They don’t bow to the emperor. And worst of all? **They claim to have the oldest, most divine knowledge on Earth.**  
  
The Greeks can’t have that. They need to **know what’s in those scrolls.** They need to get their hands on the **Jewish Torah—their sacred texts.**  
  
So Ptolemy comes up with a plan.  
  
**The Greek Trap: The Translation Project**  
  
He calls upon **Jewish scholars**—seventy of them, the best minds they have. He orders them to **translate the Hebrew texts into Greek**, so that Greek rulers, scholars, and generals can finally **access the “hidden wisdom” of the Jews.**  
  
It sounds like a win-win. The Jews get recognition, their holy texts become accessible to the greatest empire on Earth, and the Greeks get to **absorb another culture’s knowledge into their own power structure.**  
  
But something happens that the Greeks don’t see coming.  
  
**The Jewish Countermove: The Perfect Misdirection**  
  
These Jewish scholars aren’t fools. They know exactly what’s happening. They **smile, nod, and accept the assignment.** They **take the scrolls into their chambers and begin writing.**  
  
But **what they deliver back isn’t exactly what Ptolemy expects.**  
  
Here’s where it gets interesting:  
1.	**The Greeks Needed Vowels, So the Jews Gave Them What They Wanted**  
   •	The Hebrew language **didn’t write vowels.** It was a script built on consonants, where only trained Jewish scholars knew how to read it properly.   
   •	Greek, on the other hand, **relied on vowels (A, E, I, O, U).**  
   •	The Jewish translators had the **perfect opportunity** to **insert vowels where they wanted, changing subtle meanings while keeping the core text intact for themselves.**  
  
2.	**They Translated Certain Words Differently**  
   •	A **word in Hebrew could have multiple meanings**, depending on **how you vocalized it**.  
   •	Example: The Hebrew word **‘Almah’ (עלמה)** could mean **“young woman” or “virgin.”**  
   •	In Greek, they **chose** to translate it as **“virgin”**, which centuries later became the foundation for the Christian idea of the **Virgin Birth**  
  
Did they do this intentionally? **Or did they know exactly what it would lead to?**  
  
3.	**They Knew the Greeks Would Read It Without Context**  
•	The Greeks didn’t have **rabbinic oral tradition**—the spoken knowledge that **explained the deeper meanings of the Hebrew texts.**  
•	Without this **oral history**, the Greeks would be **reading the scriptures like outsiders, missing the true codes hidden within.**  
  
4.	**The Jews Let the Greeks Believe They Won**  
  
The **Greek rulers thought they had cracked Jewish wisdom.**  
•	They **stored the Septuagint in their libraries**, shared it with their scholars, and moved forward thinking they had **absorbed another culture.**  
•	But the truth? **The real Jewish knowledge stayed with the rabbis, untouched and unaltered.**  
  
**The Long-Term Consequence: Christianity Emerges**  
  
Here’s where it gets crazy.  
  
Fast forward a few hundred years. The **Romans take over.** Greek philosophy mixes with the now-widespread **Greek Septuagint**, and a new group arises—**Christians.**  
  
They read the Greek version of the Jewish texts and **reinterpret the entire thing**  
	•	They find **prophecies that, in Hebrew, might have had a different meaning**.  
•	They **see a Messiah in texts that weren’t necessarily pointing to one.**  
•	They **build an entire religion on a mistranslation that the Jews allowed to happen.**  
The Greeks and Romans thought they **controlled Jewish history**, but in reality, **they were the ones who got played.**  
  
**The Jews Won the Long Game**  
  
While the **Greeks lost their empire, and Rome eventually fell, Judaism survived**—intact, unbroken, and still controlling the original meaning of its texts.  
  
The Septuagint became the foundation for **Christianity**, but **Judaism remained separate**, holding onto its secrets while the world around it changed.  
  
The Greeks thought they were absorbing Jewish knowledge.  
Instead, they **helped create something new**, and they never saw it coming.
