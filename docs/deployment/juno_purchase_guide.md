# JUNO Token Purchase Guide
**Status**: Corrected — JUNO is Cosmos-based, not ERC-20

## The Issue
❌ JUNO does NOT exist on Ethereum/MetaMask
❌ You cannot buy it with MetaMask directly
✓ JUNO lives on the Juno Chain (Cosmos ecosystem)

## Correct Path to Buy JUNO

### Step 1: Get a Cosmos Wallet
Download ONE of these (free):
- **Keplr** (recommended): https://www.keplr.app
- **Cosmostation**: https://cosmostation.io  
- **Leap**: https://www.leapwallet.io

### Step 2: Buy USDC/USDT on Existing Exchange
Use whatever exchange you have (Kraken, Coinbase, etc):
- Send $XX to your account
- Buy USDC or USDT stablecoin
- Copy your new wallet address from Keplr

### Step 3: Transfer Stablecoin to Cosmos
Send USDC/USDT from exchange → Your Keplr address
(Choose "Cosmos" or "ATOM" network, NOT Ethereum)

### Step 4: Swap on Osmosis DEX
1. Go to https://app.osmosis.zone
2. Connect Keplr wallet
3. Trade USDC → JUNO
4. Receive JUNO in your wallet

### Step 5: Send to Blockdaemon
1. Get your Blockdaemon deposit address from their dashboard
2. In Keplr, send JUNO → Blockdaemon address
3. Confirm transaction

## Exchanges That Have JUNO Direct

| Exchange | How |
|----------|-----|
| Osmosis DEX | Swap USDC → JUNO (decentralized) |
| Kucoin | Buy JUNO/USDT directly |
| Gate.io | Has JUNO spot trading |
| Crypto.com | Check region availability |

## DO NOT
- Try to find JUNO on Ethereum (doesn't exist there)
- Look for JUNO on MetaMask-only exchanges
- Send JUNO to Ethereum addresses (will be lost)

## Remember
- JUNO native wallet = Keplr (not MetaMask)
- JUNO lives on Juno Chain (not Ethereum)
- Blockdaemon accepts JUNO but it arrives on Juno Chain
