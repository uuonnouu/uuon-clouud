# Dmension Mathematical Universe
## Turn Math Into Real 3D Content

**What is Dmension?**  
A browser-based platform that transforms mathematical formulas into interactive 3D models. No software to install, no steep learning curve — just open and explore thousands of mathematical shapes instantly.
