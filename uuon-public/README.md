## What Is UUON?

UUON is a **Mathematical Operating System (MOS)** — a parametric geometry engine where shapes are blockchain proofs.

**The shapes are not visualizations. They are the algorithms expressed as geometry.**

Every cryptographic primitive, quantum gate, and mathematical function used in modern computing has a corresponding parametric surface in the Δmension registry. These surfaces are Instant-NGP compatible NeRF assets — they carry the equation, the proof, and the authorship record simultaneously.

### Geometric Proof of Work

Validators compute a valid parametric surface within defined mathematical bounds. The surface *is* the proof. Validity is deterministically verifiable by all nodes. Difficulty adjusts by tightening tolerance on surface equation accuracy — not by increasing hash iterations.
