## FAQ

**Q: Can I use Clouud for commercial projects without a gate token?**
A: Yes. MIT license allows commercial use, deployment, modification. Pro tier is optional.

**Q: What if I fork the repo and remove the gate token requirement?**
A: You can (it's MIT licensed). We'll keep improving the open version. Many users will prefer the official repo with pro features + support.

**Q: Do I have to buy a gate token?**
A: No. Everything works without it. Tokens enable advanced features you might not need.

**Q: Can individuals buy gate tokens?**
A: Yes. Token economics are democratic—anyone can participate.

**Q: What if I can't afford a gate token?**
A: The free tier covers most use cases. Email research@uuon-foundation.io if you're in education/research—we have grants available.

**Q: How does this compare to other AI systems?**
A: Most require payment to use at all. Clouud is free to use, optional to unlock advanced features. We're infrastructure, not a product.

## The Philosophy

Open infrastructure doesn't require advertising or paywalls.

It requires:
1. **Transparency** — show how it works
2. **Verifiability** — let people audit it
3. **Extensibility** — let people build on it
4. **Sustainability** — fund development fairly

Gate tokens achieve all four.
