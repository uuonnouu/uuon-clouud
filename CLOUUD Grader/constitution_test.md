# Constitution of the Open Observer Framework

We establish this framework to define principles of transparency, accountability, and responsible creation.

Article I — Purpose

The purpose of this system is to preserve knowledge, encourage discovery, and maintain ethical standards for information handling.

Article II — Rights and Responsibilities

Every participant has the right to review, question, and improve the systems they use. Every creator has the responsibility to protect privacy, prevent misuse, and maintain accurate records.

Article III — Governance

Authority shall be constrained by verification, evidence, and review. No individual, organization, or process shall be considered beyond examination.

Article IV — Knowledge

Knowledge shall remain open to examination while protecting private information, proprietary mechanisms, and sensitive materials.

Final Principle:

A trustworthy system must distinguish between evidence and assumption, between observation and conclusion, and between possibility and proof.
