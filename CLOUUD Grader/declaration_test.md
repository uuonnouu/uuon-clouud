# Declaration of Independent Systems

We establish this declaration as a statement of principles.

We believe that knowledge should be examined through reason, evidence, and open inquiry.

Every individual should have the ability to create, explore, question, and improve ideas.

Power should remain accountable to verification.

Innovation should serve understanding and responsible progress.

A system should be judged by its actions, its transparency, and its ability to correct mistakes.

This declaration represents a commitment to cooperation, discovery, and the responsible advancement of technology.
