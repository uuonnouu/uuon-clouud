Core Concepts and Modeling Focus
The UUON Foundation's portfolio is built on "Intelligent Geometry"—the idea that 3D forms should be mathematically coherent and geodetically anchored. 
	•	Lattice Structures & Nodal Topography: They create intricate 3D models like the Face-Centered Cubic (FCC) Crystal Lattice, demonstrating how nodes (vertices) and edges (lattice vectors) create high-efficiency structural frameworks.
	•	Mathematical Surfaces: Their models include complex topologies such as Riemannian Volume Form Surfaces, Kuen Surfaces, and Cross Caps. These visualize how space-time or curved manifolds can be represented through geometry.
	•	Geodesic Bridges: A major theme in their work is "bridging" concepts. They use the Geodesic Equation to model the shortest paths on curved surfaces, which informs their bridge designs. This approach allows for structures that are structurally smooth and "quantum-orbital" in appearance. 

Innovative "Intelligent" Geometry

	•	Geodetic Mapping: They have developed unique representations of Earth, such as the u²-based Earth-shell hologram, which merges electron-shell topology with geodetic mapping.

	•	Interactivity: Their Sketchfab models are often designed to be "scrolled inside," allowing users to see effects like gravitational lensing—the warping of space-time visualized through 3D geometry.
